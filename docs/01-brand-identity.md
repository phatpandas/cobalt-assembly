# Cobalt Assembly — Brand Identity

## Status

**Working brand direction:** Cobalt Assembly

The name combines material precision with coordinated construction. It is intended to feel couture-tech, engineered, and credible in enterprise settings. Trademark and domain availability have not been screened.

## Positioning

> Cobalt Assembly is a couture-grade AI operating layer for modern digital businesses—uniting elevated templates, focused micro-SaaS tools, and intelligent content systems into one motion-driven ecosystem.

## Competitive angle

Cobalt Assembly is not a disconnected asset marketplace or a collection of generic AI wrappers. It supplies an integrated operating environment where templates, tools, and learning products lead naturally to the next useful business outcome. The difference is system cohesion combined with couture-grade utility and enterprise-caliber clarity.

## Aesthetic direction — Sculpted Luminous Precision

- White-studio compositions, editorial whitespace, and calibrated asymmetry.
- Blue and silver behave as material finishes rather than loud decorative colors.
- Motion feels sculpted: orbital traces, precision reveals, and responsive product states.
- Interfaces prioritize composure, hierarchy, and legibility over spectacle.
- AI is an embedded operating layer, never a novelty or visual gimmick.

| `ink-blue` | `#26344B` | Accessible text and icons only |
| `line` | `#DEE4EC` | Hairline borders and control boundaries |

**Signature gradient:** `#4A78FF → #7FB2FF`.

Use the gradient as a restrained spectral edge, soft shader wash, or moving orbital trace. Backgrounds remain white or near-white. Dark themes, black fills, charcoal panels, and dark hero sections are out of bounds.

## Voice

Cobalt Assembly sounds lucid, elegant, technically fluent, quietly ambitious, outcome-first, and couture-refined.

- Say: “Turn expertise into an operating asset.”
- Say: “A clearer system for the next stage of growth.”
- Say: “Built for work that deserves to endure.”
- Avoid hype, urgency theatre, vague AI claims, and slang-heavy growth language.

## Brand rules

- Lead with the outcome, then explain the system.
- Cobalt is directional; silver is structural; white is the luxury field.
- No purple-led palette, neon cyberpunk treatment, or dark visual mode.
- Use motion to reveal state, sequence, or relationship—never merely to decorate.
